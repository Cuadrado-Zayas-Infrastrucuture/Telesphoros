# ASSESSMENT-ON accepts a break-glass image record (032 S3, PR 3c-3b)

Date: 2026-11-04

`ASSESSMENT-ON` (PR 3c-3a) accepted only a signature record for the running image, the same check as `FENCE-OPEN`. Synthetic staging cannot produce one: its service control policy denies a GitHub OIDC provider, so every staging image has been unsigned break-glass unless the staging-only signing door (`image_release_human_principals`) is opened, and the recipe cannot add that variable to the hashed `current.tfvars`. So the switch could never have been turned on. On the founder's go-ahead of 2026-11-04 it now also accepts a break-glass record: environment `staging`, the exact digest of the running image and nothing else, and a written reason of 10 to 300 characters on one line, checked by the recorder's own validator (`validReason` in `verify-image.mjs`, imported and not copied). `FENCE-OPEN` still requires a signature; production is untouched and the recorder itself refuses break-glass there.

What changed: `fence-image-record.mjs` gains `coversAssessmentImage` and an `--allow-break-glass` option on its command line; `plan.sh` and `apply.sh` pass the option for `ASSESSMENT-ON` only, so the record is checked again at apply; [synthetic-assessment-switch.md](../ops/synthetic-assessment-switch.md) has a section on the unsigned image and what it does not give; `staging-release.md` and the plan table follow.

Verification, Node 22.22.0: `fence-vars.test.mjs` 12 of 12 (four new tests, among them 23 ways a record can be too weak and a command-line test that proves a refusal prints nothing from the record), `release-scripts.test.mjs` 7 of 7 (it plans and applies `ASSESSMENT-ON` on a break-glass record, refuses one without a reason, for another image or for production, and shows the medication fence refusing a break-glass record at plan and at apply); `test:infra` 392 of 392 and `test:ci-config` 221 of 221. Eleven deliberate breaks of the new gate, its command line and the two shell call sites each failed the test meant to catch them (one first survived, because the staging-image pattern only does unique work when a record and an image agree with each other; that case is now tested) and every file was restored byte-identical.

Not done: nothing was planned, applied or released against staging. A break-glass record proves a person recorded this digest on purpose with a reason; it does not prove the image is what CI built beyond the same-commit CI `image` job the image step requires, and it leaves no signature for a later reader. The founder-run release (PR 3c-3) is next.
