# Phenoconversion notice: the N3 plan (spec 045)

Date: 2026-11-22

A plan, `specs/045-phenoconversion-notice/plan-slice-n3.md`, for showing N2's notices on the genomic profile screen in plain words:
"lowers" and "raises" on the row, and the FDA's terms and source in the evidence. It also covers a line, always shown, that says what the
check covers or that it is not running yet. The draft wording is the founders' to sign (P-D6). Three decisions were put to them: N3-D1, the
glossary entry; N3-D2, the always-visible line; N3-D3, everyday verbs first. The founder approved all three as recommended on
2026-11-23. No code, setting or knowledge change.
