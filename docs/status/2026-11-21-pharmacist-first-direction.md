# Product direction: pharmacist first, a worklist home, chat as the help assistant (plan only)

Date: 2026-11-21

A draft plan, [pharmacist-first-product-direction.md](../design/plans/pharmacist-first-product-direction.md), records four founder
decisions of 2026-11-21: the first user is a pharmacist-led PGx service (N1); the clinical home is a worklist by role (N2); chat becomes
the product's help assistant for features, how-tos, FAQs and policies, never the clinical interface, replacing the 2026-11-02 direction
that chat is the primary clinical interface (N3); and that assistant starts with curated answers and no model, to ship faster and prove the
concept, with a model added later as the business lane of the existing agentic design (N4). It summarizes research on competitors, maps the product's edge against what the repository has today, and proposes
slices A1 to A4, B1 and B2, each needing its own spec and approval. Open decisions F1 to F5 wait on the founder. No code, migration,
setting, knowledge file or clinical hold changes, and no model, host or spend is approved.
