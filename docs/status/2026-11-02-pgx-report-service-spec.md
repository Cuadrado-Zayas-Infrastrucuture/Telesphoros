# PGx laboratory report and assessment service proposal

Date: 2026-11-02

[Spec 032](../../specs/032-pgx-report-service/spec.md) translates the supplied PGx research into proposed laboratory/assessment contracts, a 21-gene-plus-locus catalogue with 28 candidate medication pathways, partner procurement requirements and 19 synthetic acceptance scenarios. It preserves laboratory findings separately from contextual assessments, retains uncertainty and amendments, and specifies reviewed knowledge snapshots and interim-conflict handling. The plan maps existing decisions and follow-up reminders into the proposed workflow.

Design only: the coverage matrix is not an exhaustive CPIC pair/cell audit; the new scenarios are unexecuted requirements. No reader engine, clinical content, migration, runtime behavior or clinical gate changes. Implementation, assay qualification and clinical activation remain separate work.
