# Phenoconversion notice on the genomic profile screen (spec 045 N3)

Date: 2026-11-23

The genomic profile screen shows the notices slice N2 sends. On a medication's row, the drug checked before prescribing and the gene's card,
in everyday words: "A medication this patient takes can change this. [Medication], which this patient takes, strongly lowers [gene] activity.
The guidance above is based on the genes alone and does not take this into account." The source's own terms, the list, its version, table
and row, and an `https`-only link are under "Why, and the evidence". A line always shown under the medications heading says what the check
covers, or that it is not checked yet, and an ⓘ gives the full "About this check" (N3-D2).

All the wording is **draft, awaiting the founders' signature (P-D6)**, English only. The glossary entry waits for the glossary (N3-D1).
On staging no list is loaded, so the screen says "not checked yet". Frontend only: no backend, schema, route, setting or knowledge change.
