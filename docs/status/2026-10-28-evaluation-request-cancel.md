# Feature: the requester can cancel an evaluation request that has not started (H4 slice 2, local)

Date: 2026-10-28

Slice 2 of spec 007 (H4, asynchronous evaluation requests), approved by the founder on 2026-10-27 with D2 to D4 as recommended: cancel is included, only for a request no worker has started, only by the person who queued it, and recorded in a new append-only table. On 2026-10-28 the founder chose to cut this slice off `main` without the route, so it does not stack on the unmerged slice 1; `POST /evaluation-requests/:id/cancel` moves to slice 3 with the review page's Cancel button.

Changed: migration 0054 adds `evaluation_request_cancellations` (who cancelled which request, and when; append-only, forced tenant row-level security, readable and insertable by the application only) and `evaluation_job_cancel(job, request)`, a narrow definer function owned by the queue's owner role. It cancels one evaluation-request job of the bound organization, queued by the person acting, while it is `queued` or `retry`, under a `FOR UPDATE` row lock; otherwise it refuses (EC001 once the job has started). A cancellation is written with one insert, whose check requires the person acting to be the one named, to hold a write role now, and the job helper to succeed; the record and the job's cancellation are stored together or not at all. A claim takes rows `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` (migration 0023), so a cancel and a claim never both succeed. `EvaluationRequestsService.cancel` returns the request; the status read gains the state `cancelled`, also for a job an operator cancelled with the jobs CLI (no person recorded then). 409 `not-cancellable` for a started or finished request, 403 for anyone but the requester, 404 for another organization or an unknown request. The down migration refuses while any cancellation exists, run as the restricted migrator.

Proved on real PostgreSQL with public fixture D01 (`test/evaluation-request-cancel.e2e-spec.ts`, and a case in `test/db/down-guards-as-migrator.pg-spec.ts`): a queued and a retrying request cancel, and a cancelled one is never handed to a worker nor evaluated; running, dead, completed and already-cancelled requests are refused and keep their state; a colleague, a reader, and the requester after losing their write role are refused, with the requester's own cancel as the control; another organization and an unknown id get 404; direct writes are refused (a record for someone else's request, a record naming another person, an update, delete or truncate, and an application update of the job), and a colleague calling the job helper directly is refused while the requester's own call goes through. Twelve rounds of a concurrent cancel and claim each have exactly one winner (9 cancels and 3 claims won in each full-loop tier). Eleven mutations were each caught; see [the verification record](../verification/evaluation-request-cancel.md).

Not changed: no route (slice 3), the review page (slice 3), the H3 worker and its binding to the requesting person, `PRODUCTION_JOBS`, the worker app, schedules, infrastructure and CI workflows, the jobs CLI's own cancel, clinical rules, holds and goldens, any model or provider. A running read cannot be cancelled by its requester; that needs a fence the worker checks (spec 007, out of scope).

Local only until the founder says push.
