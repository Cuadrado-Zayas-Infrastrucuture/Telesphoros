# Spec 053 slice C1: the Help drawer is a conversation

Date: 2026-11-26

Help, in the sidebar, is now a conversation ([plan](../../specs/053-help-chat/plan-slice-c1.md), approved with C1-D1 and C1-D2 as
recommended). The person types a question and presses Enter. Help answers under it with one of three things:
- the best article or glossary word, quoted whole with its links and its draft line, and up to two more under **Also in Help**;
- the fixed refusal, for a question about a patient, decided before any search;
- "No article answers that exactly" and the closest three.

A suggested article adds its answer to the conversation. The last 20 exchanges are kept, and all of them are cleared when the drawer closes.

There is no model, route, setting or new content; the engine (`lib/helpSearch.ts`) is unchanged. `lib/helpConversation.ts` holds the rule
order, refusal first, for C2's fallback to reuse. Nothing typed is sent, stored or put in the address, and the tests carried over from spec
051 still assert it.
