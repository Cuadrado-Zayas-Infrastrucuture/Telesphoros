## Found and fixed during verification

- **Horizontal scroll at 375 px.** The first full browser run failed three mobile cases (79 passed, 3 failed: the two landing specs and `foundation.spec.ts` "fits the viewport"). The patient-to-file fragment's chips do not wrap, so it widened its row's text column to 422 px. On phones the fragment now stacks with a vertical wire, and the row's text column may shrink (`min-width: 0`).
- **Horizontal scroll at 375 px with 200% text.** The next run failed one case: the same chips ran to 458 px at 200% text. The chips' text now wraps and they may shrink. Measured at 375 px after the fix: no overflow at 100% or at 200%.
- **Success colour.** "Server verified" in the file-status fragment first rendered in the muted ink because `span:last-child` outranked the class selector. Fixed by raising the selector's specificity.
