## Limits

Results are fixed, copied demonstration data. They are not extracted, computed, clinically validated or generated by a model. The real reader, independent review persistence and engine orchestration remain separate work. Default backend/worker service tiers were not selected without their test configuration; the owned demo exercised its own actual local storage path. Local Moto is not AWS/IAM/custody proof. No hosted run, clinical approval, deployment, model call, push or PR is established here.
