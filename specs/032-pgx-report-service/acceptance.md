# Synthetic acceptance specification

**Target acceptance specification: the full cases below are not yet implemented or executed.** S1 validates synthetic contracts; S2a exercises only stored-source inspection and patient-link review ([bounded evidence](../../docs/status/2026-11-02-pgx-structured-intake.md)), not baseline B or assessment release. They define observable outcomes for future tests; they are not clinical dosing oracles. Use invented patients, laboratory IDs and report bytes. Generic rules use GENEA/GENEB as in existing fixtures. Cases mentioning real genes describe uncertainty/scope behavior; exact clinical expected cells require an independent qualified reviewer before implementation. Do not derive expected outputs from the code being tested.

Baseline B: authenticated synthetic laboratory; tenant T1/patient P1; final report R1 revision 1; exact PDF/JSON pair; manifest M1 with a tested and resolved GENEA finding F1; independent acceptance; context X1; reviewed synthetic rule K1 for drug D1; all prerequisites present. All artifacts remain synthetic and `clinicalUse=not-approved`, `actionable=false`. Baseline identifiers are scenario labels, not valid production DTOs.

## Required evidence by boundary

Import and authority cases require existing real PostgreSQL application-role/RLS and S3-emulator tiers, plus exact-version mismatch and transaction-failure controls. UI cases require real browser checks of chat upload, review dialog, source details and patient follow-up/reminders. Clinical source cases require independent pharmacist-authored expected cells and named review. FHIR requires profile/terminology validation; assays require laboratory analytical validation. A successful unit test cannot substitute for any of these.

Keep source fixtures separate from expected outcomes, and include mutation controls that remove a prerequisite, swap a patient, change a source version or suppress a limitation. Record test counts, skips, source versions, tool versions, checkout and reviewer. Existing reader-engine-absent skips remain explicit missing evidence.

## Partial lineage evidence (S2b1)

The [revision inspection tests](../../packages/shared-types/src/synthetic-pgx-lineage.test.ts) cover pure classification relevant to A09/A10: identical uploads versus changed revision content, complete/predecessor-linked histories, missing/branched revisions, issuer identity reuse, scope drift and latest-record withdrawal. They preserve source references and refuse automatic fallback or historical finding backfill. They do **not** establish idempotent persistence, issuer authentication, independent acceptance, commit-time authority/freshness, affected-assessment tasks or assessment release. Those API/database/browser acceptance cases above remain required for the later integrated path.

## Stored evidence boundary (S2b2)

The [stored-record API cases](../../apps/backend/test/structured-report-records.e2e-spec.ts) exercise the integrated portion of A09/A10/A16 against real PostgreSQL/S3: source-bound save/replay/concurrency, distinct duplicate provenance, held conflicting/orphan revisions, complete amendment chains without historical finding backfill, issuer identity collisions across patients, changed reviews/deleted sources, unavailable pinned bytes, revoked authors, stale patient bindings, bounded complete history, RLS finding reads, append-only/event constraints, commit-time rollback and ledger corruption detection. The migration guard is exercised as the non-superuser migrator, including refusal with FORCE RLS and reversal/reapplication when empty.

The [browser path](../../apps/frontend/storage-e2e/structured-preview.spec.ts) uploads from chat, creates/selects a patient, records the independent match, saves findings, and reopens persisted history at mobile/desktop sizes. Interface cases also require a fresh read after an uncertain write. These checks establish synthetic source persistence and candidate classification only. They do not satisfy the remaining A10 requirements for analytical acceptance, affected-assessment review tasks or released/current assessments, nor authenticate the laboratory or validate clinical calls.

## Findings review boundary (S2c)

The [review and resolution API cases](../../apps/backend/test/structured-findings-review.e2e-spec.ts) extend A09/A10/A16 against real PostgreSQL/S3: independent per-finding decisions bound to the exact revision, source hash/version and patient-link decision; the saver and uploader refused (API and direct SQL); role, tenant and strict-body refusals; append-only history where the newest decision wins; uncertainty left unchanged by an acceptance; no patient-link write; held, superseded and not-selected revisions closed to review; an explicit, exact-set conflict resolution that keeps every source, never revives a superseded revision, and goes stale when another source arrives; commit-time recheck and rollback, revoked reviewer, append-only and ledger-verifier corruption. Two guard-removal controls (independence trigger, deferred commit-time recheck) failed the suite as intended.

The browser path adds an independent third person reviewing the saved findings at mobile and desktop sizes. Conflict resolution is covered by component and API cases, not by a browser flow. These checks record that stored evidence matches its source. They do not establish analytical acceptance, laboratory authentication, an assessment, a current patient profile or any clinical use, and A10's affected-assessment tasks remain open.


## Synthetic assessment boundary (S3, 2b)

The [assessment API cases](../../apps/backend/test/structured-assessment.e2e-spec.ts) and [plan cases](../../apps/backend/src/lab-reports/structured-assessment.spec.ts) cover the integrated part of A07, A08 and three of A19's four results against real PostgreSQL/S3, with expected cells written by hand from [the synthetic structured knowledge set](../../data/fixtures/knowledge/synthetic-structured/README.md): a complete reviewed revision selects the verified combined cell; an explicit no-recommendation cell, a combination with no cell and an unverified cell stay distinguishable and expose no wording they must not; a gene that was not tested holds the table that needs it with no fallback to a narrower row, while a drug that needs only the tested gene still answers; a disputed cell is held with its citation and every other cell is unaffected; unreviewed, held, rejected, uncertain, non-diplotype, extra-copy, preliminary and conflicting sources are held without quoting the report. Refused without storing: no verified patient link, a report that is not structured, no stored revision, stale evidence, a revision not under review, an origin that cannot be named, a missing build version. Fifteen guard-removal controls and a gene-map drift control fail the suites as intended.

A13, A19's unknown-drug result, A12, A18 and any rendering are not covered. No route or page can request an assessment yet, and staging cannot store one until the synthetic-staging profile names the data origin there. These cases establish a synthetic assessment from stored, independently reviewed evidence only; they do not establish any clinical use.
